## A. Introduction

*Inside Out* is an animated comedy-drama film produced by **Pixar Animation Studios** and released by **Walt Disney Pictures** in 2015. The movie explores the emotions inside the mind of an 11-year-old girl named Riley as she adjusts to a new life in San Francisco. It focuses on the importance of understanding and accepting different emotions, especially during difficult changes in life.

## B. Synopsis

*Inside Out* tells the story of an 11-year-old girl named Riley who moves from Minnesota to San Francisco with her parents.

Like everyone else, Riley has **five core emotions** living inside her mind:

- 🟡 **Joy** – Happiness and positivity.
- 🔵 **Sadness** – Sorrow and empathy.
- 🔴 **Anger** – Frustration and irritation.
- 🟣 **Fear** – Caution and protection.
- 🟢 **Disgust** – Protection from unpleasant situations.

These emotions help guide her thoughts, memories, and actions as she experiences different situations in her daily life.

Joy tries her best to keep Riley happy, but things become complicated when Joy and Sadness accidentally get lost outside the headquarters of Riley's mind. Without them, Riley struggles to understand and express her feelings.

Meanwhile, Joy and Sadness go on an adventure to find their way back to headquarters, meeting different parts of Riley's personality along the way.

During their journey, Joy realizes that sadness is also an important emotion because it allows people to express their feelings and receive comfort from others.

Joy and Sadness return to headquarters, helping Riley understand her emotions and communicate honestly with her parents.

> 💡 **Main Lesson:** All emotions have a purpose, and being sad is a normal part of growing up.

## C. Characters

Here are seven important characters in the movie and their descriptions:

### Main Characters

-  **Joy** – The main emotion who always tries to make Riley happy. She is energetic, optimistic, and believes that happiness is the most important feeling.
-  **Sadness** – A blue-colored emotion who represents sorrow and empathy. She plays an important role in Riley's emotional well-being.
-  **Anger** – A red-colored emotion who represents frustration and irritation. He reacts strongly whenever things seem unfair or do not go as planned.
-  **Fear** – A purple-colored emotion responsible for protecting Riley from danger. He is cautious and constantly worries about possible risks.
-  **Disgust** – A green-colored emotion who protects Riley from unpleasant things. She helps Riley avoid things that might be embarrassing, unhealthy, or socially unacceptable.

### Other Important Characters

-  **Riley Andersen** – The 11-year-old girl whose emotions control the headquarters inside her mind. She struggles with adjusting to her family's move to a new city.
-  **Bing Bong** – Riley's imaginary friend from her childhood. He helps Joy and Sadness during their journey through Riley's mind.

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie

I chose *Inside Out* because it is an entertaining and meaningful movie that teaches important lessons about emotions and mental well-being. I like how the story presents emotions as characters, making it easier for viewers to understand what happens inside the human mind. It is also relatable because everyone experiences happiness, sadness, anger, fear, and other emotions in everyday life.

### 3 Things I Liked About the Movie

1. **Meaningful Storyline**  
   I liked how the movie teaches us that sadness is not a weakness. It shows that expressing our feelings and accepting our emotions are important parts of life.

2. **Creative Animation and Characters**  
   The colorful world inside Riley's mind is imaginative and visually entertaining. Each emotion has a unique personality, appearance, and way of reacting to situations.

3. **Emotional and Relatable Scenes**  
   Some scenes are touching and realistic, especially when Riley struggles with adjusting to her new environment. These moments remind viewers that growing up and experiencing changes can be difficult.
